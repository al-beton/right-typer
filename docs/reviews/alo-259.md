# ALO-259 · round two

[Review gallery](https://al-beton.github.io/right-typer-previews/pr-30/wordmarks.html)

Al selected **Quiet underline** after round two and requested a matching favicon.
The recommended pairing is RT with two separate five-colour underlines, shown
prominently at the top. Earlier, Al clarified that the sequence must
have **two greens and two turquoises**, like the keyboard. This revision replaces
the eight-finger cycle with the ten visible Standard QWERTY columns:

| Keyboard column | q    | w      | e      | r     | t     | y         | u         | i    | o      | p    |
| --------------- | ---- | ------ | ------ | ----- | ----- | --------- | --------- | ---- | ------ | ---- |
| Right Typer     | R    | i      | g      | h     | t     | T         | y         | p    | e      | r    |
| Colour          | rose | orange | yellow | green | green | turquoise | turquoise | blue | purple | pink |

These are existing CSS keyboard tokens. The browser test compares every column
against the actual rendered keyboard. This is a sequential branding treatment,
not instructions for fingering the letters in the name. Space is separate; an
optional thumb-colour treatment never replaces the repeated index columns.

Bright Typer remains a review-only alternative: its extra initial B shares the
first rose column with r, preserving all ten columns and the repeated greens and
turquoises. The project is still Right Typer.

## Three refinements

1. **Sequential:** outlined pastel letters in the ten-column sequence.
2. **Quiet underline:** high-contrast ink letters, a colour segment per letter.
3. **Keyboard underline:** ink letters with one even ten-column colour line.

All appear enlarged and at the real 22px header size. “Try in header” updates a
combined header/tab specimen. The ink text has 14.78:1 contrast on #fafafa;
pastel fills alone remain low-contrast (1.27–1.85:1), so Sequential has an ink outline.

## Matching favicon candidates

- **RT underline:** ink monogram with five colours under R and five under T,
  matching the two word underlines.
- **Index pair:** RT over two green and two turquoise columns.
- **Quiet key:** compact T/key outline with the ten-column colour line.

The gallery shows 64px, native 32px/16px, and light/dark tab specimens. “Try in tab”
changes this page's real favicon. Each candidate has editable SVG, PNG exports at
16/32/48px, and a multi-size ICO; download links are in the gallery. RT candidates
remain Right Typer initials when the alternate display name is selected.

Assets live in `public/wordmarks/`. Regenerate with `node scripts/wordmark-icons.mjs`
after installing dependencies and Playwright Chromium. The generator reads the
keyboard CSS tokens and uses vector paths, with no external fonts or raster art.

`name=bright`, `style=seq|ink|rail`, `icon=rt|split|key`, and `thumbs=1` in the review
URL preserve the comparison across reloads. No settings or localStorage writes,
no camera startup, and no change to the normal app's logo/favicon.
